Project purpose

LakeWatch Laguna is an academic environmental information platform for exploring published Laguna de Bay monitoring measurements through interactive maps, station profiles, charts, and source-labeled reports.

It helps visitors locate monitoring areas, understand water-quality parameters, compare historical observations, and examine supporting environmental context.

Data status: This is a publicly accessible website with shared data updates, not an official live LLDA water-quality sensor feed.

▤

  • Explore mapped station areas and historical readings
  • Interpret six parameters and compare station results
  • Inspect annual historical trends and forecasting limits
  • Browse documented incidents and weather context
  • Export source-labeled PDF reports and filtered CSV
  • Submit verified updates through protected Manage Data

Data stewardship

Manage Data supports manual entry, CSV/Excel file import and import from a direct data URL. Manual/file observations may cite an online publication or a printed/offline document.

Administrator-approved observations are stored in a shared Render PostgreSQL database. Citations and review information should be checked before publication; offline citations do not upload document scans.

Readings below reporting limits (for example, <0.1) retain their original qualifiers. Historical values are not treated as real-time measurements.

↗

Environmental intelligence

Parameter interpretation is explanatory, not a regulatory certification. Multi-station comparison uses matched reporting periods, and forecasting requires adequate comparable observations.

Administrator-documented incident reports are separate from laboratory measurements. Lake Context presents Open-Meteo rainfall and air temperature separately from in-lake monitoring.

Model forecasts are demonstrative estimates, not official LLDA predictions.

≋

Data methodology & limitations

Open the details to review record coverage, coordinate confidence and station chronology.

Read historical coverage, station establishment evidence and map accuracy

The monthly explorer preserves 186 historical LLDA quarterly-report measurements: five-station Q4 2023 and June 2026 data from 16 reported stations. In addition, 21 reported lake-wide BOD/TSS annual averages for 1990–2000 (ILEC) and 48 station annual averages for 2009–2012 (LLDA) are kept in a separate historical file, giving 255 distinct reported values in total. This is not a live monitoring API. The site preserves each reading’s source period and units. Values such as <0.1 are preserved as detection-limit labels rather than incorrectly assigned exact numeric values. Only the 16 map-pinnable reporting stations have approximate coordinates. Identifiers III, VI, VII are documented in the directory with no speculative map markers; Station VII conflicts across the 2017 LLDA map and table. Official establishment date is unknown except inclusion years explicitly documented as 2012 and 2021. Marker coordinates remain approximate visual estimates and must never be represented as official sampling GPS positions. The outline loads OpenStreetMap relation geometry when possible and otherwise falls back to an explicitly labeled schematic.

LLDA has conducted water monitoring since 1973; that year is not the founding date of every station. I, II, IV, V and VIII were active by at least 2009, with no verified individual establishment dates. LLDA documents four additional stations XV–XVIII added in 2012; PSA/LLDA documents XIII and XIX–XXIII added in 2021. Station XXIV appears in the June 2026 roster but its exact founding date has not been established. Identifiers III, VI and VII are shown as unverified registry references only; the 2017 LLDA annual table calls South Bay VII while its maps elsewhere use VIII. Their independent physical existence and precise sites are not confirmed.
