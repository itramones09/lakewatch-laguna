**What is known about all locations?** The 16 pins correspond to published 2026 *lake* monitoring areas; these are not lakeshore physical buildings. Sampling stations may be recurring geographic positions rather than constructed facilities. Their original establishment date must come from LLDA archived records; first appearance is not proof of initial establishment. There is no authoritative evidence in the reviewed sources of a separate physical station at III or VI, or two distinct South Bay sampling positions for VII and VIII. We therefore do not place speculative pins for them.
