# Historical archive — method and verification

## Count reconciliation

| Type | Years | Parameter coverage | Number of reported values | Primary evidence |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Lake-wide annual averages | 1990–2000 | 11 BOD + 10 TSS | 21 | ILEC, Laguna de Bay, Table 3, p. 234 |
| Station-specific annual averages | 2009–2012 | 24 station-years × 2 (BOD, TSS) | 48 | LLDA Annual Report 2009–2012, pp. 9 and 57 |
| Station-specific monthly observations | Oct–Dec 2023 and Jun 2026 | 90 + 96 values | 186 | LLDA Q4 2023 and Q2 2026 reports |
| **TOTAL** | **1990–June 2026 (not continuous)** | | **255** | |

The archive shows **no interpolated results**. `null` means **not reported in the chosen source**. In monthly records, strings such as `<0.1` mean below a detection/reporting threshold, and remain separate from numeric values.

## Statistical caveats

The 1990–2000 series is not site-specific. Do not present its lake-wide aggregate as a reading at Station I, II, or any other specific station. The 2009–2012 observations are station-specific annual averages, and the 2023–2026 values are monthly station measurements, so the series are displayed independently. The full date axis has gaps; connecting lines are limited to valid same-method contiguous observations and excluded across gaps.

No historical annual measurement is inserted into the monthly 186-record Data Explorer because it would confuse frequency, number of records, and station coverage. The historical chart has its own accessible data table with source links.
