# LakeWatch Laguna — Suggested Six-Minute Demonstration

**Presented by:** Ian Titus F. Ramones and Dycyn T. Alvaro  
**WITM 101 Final Examination — MIT, LSPU San Pablo City Campus**

| Time | Demonstration | Talking points |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:40 | Overview | Introduce the problem, the lake, both developers and why readings need geographic context |
| 0:40–1:50 | Lake Map | Zoom/pan; show the lake boundary; choose a station using a tag or dropdown; change month and parameter |
| 1:50–2:40 | Monitoring Stations | Switch Active/Archived; search; open station profile and dated six-parameter readings |
| 2:40–3:50 | Analytics | Compare station values, change a parameter, show a historical series and explain gaps |
| 3:50–4:35 | Data Explorer | Filter by parameter and station; sort; export a filtered CSV |
| 4:35–5:15 | Responsive design | Switch Light/Dark/System and show a mobile-width layout |
| 5:15–6:00 | About and conclusion | Identify LLDA/ILEC sources, coordinate limitations and future improvements |

## Essential accuracy statements

- "These readings are derived from selected published records; they are not live sensor values."
- "Our Active and Archived station views are based on whether a June 2026 measurement is present, not confirmation of agency closure."
- "The earliest annual series and later station-month records have different statistical aggregation, so we do not connect them into one continuous trend."
- "Station coordinates are published research references or approximate placements, not LLDA-certified GPS fixes."
- "Each parameter has its own color, but those colors do not rate water safety."

## Before presenting

Open the application with **Live Server**, not `file:///`. Check map tile loading, the station locator, the analytics chart, CSV export and your internet connection. Keep the extracted project directory available as a fallback if hosting is unavailable.
